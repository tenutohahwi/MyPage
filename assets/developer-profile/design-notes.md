Use generous negative space. At center-left, small bold wordmark HAHWI with small cobalt blue dot. Beneath it, very large, beautifully typeset Korean bold sans serif headline EXACTLY these words, on two balanced lines: "일상에 필요한" then "작은 편리함". The headline should occupy the central safe area and be legible on mobile. Right side: a small, thoughtful abstract sculptural cluster of four smooth rounded tiles in soft blue, ivory and mint, carrying simple debossed symbols of a bell, photo frame, notebook and small sparkle, subtle natural shadows, restrained not cluttered. Include a small geometric H monogram near HAHWI: two navy vertical rounded bars connected by cobalt crossbar with tiny cobalt dot near upper right. Keep all text and principal graphic well inside the central 70% width and central 60% height to protect cropping. No text besides HAHWI and the exact Korean headline. No dollar signs, no coins, no generic finance imagery, no repeating dots, no gradients behind typography, no fine print, no watermark.
